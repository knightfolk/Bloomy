# Native polish completion matrix — October 2, 2026

The [active plan](APP_POLISH_OPTIMIZATION_PLAN.md) remains open. This is an
index of recorded evidence, not completion of the full goal. **Covered** applies
only to the named bounded assertion; **partial** means requirements remain;
**open** means absent or inconclusive proof. Every route below is partial overall.

Rendered/AX observations predominantly use isolated AppKit fixtures with
production views, synthetic data, isolated preferences and documented inert
substitutions. Unit/source evidence is distinguished. Compact usually means
800 × 560; wide means 1280 × 900. Older observations cover their recorded
component snapshot, not subsequent edits. Reviews contain provenance and limits.
The linked reviews record the inspections; this matrix is their evidence index.
