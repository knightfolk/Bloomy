# Bloomy v1.9.11

Action History now reliably recognizes previously saved jobs with fractional timestamps after a refresh or restart. A successful job retry clears its temporary save warning while preserving warnings about missing app actions.

The new history keeps nudge attempts and outcomes, model/provider actions, and reported jobs and base rewards. Job details include model, earning ID, tokens, and exact earnings to six decimal places. Open **Diagnostics → Action History** to browse or search. History is stored locally for up to 30 days and 5,000 records; earlier app actions cannot be reconstructed.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.
