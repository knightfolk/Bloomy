# Darkbloom Control v1.9.0

- Redesign the menu-bar popup around your Mac's name, GPU utilization, fan readings
  and controls, and a compact two-column grid of advertised and available models.
- Use shared model cards with family icons, short names, full names on hover,
  serving state, measured speed, and clearly labeled earnings when available.
- Make Overview denser, standardize Activity filters, and reorganize Opportunity
  into compact comparison cards with detailed reasoning available on demand.
- Let Models expand to three columns when the window has enough room.
- Rework Health & Logs with clearer source status, a selectable log table,
  responsive filters, and full event details.
- Bring earnings and estimated electricity costs together while keeping missing
  measurements and observation coverage explicit.

This update changes presentation without moving models or changing provider,
fan, model-selection, or automatic-update preferences. The iOS companion and
remote-control service remain coming soon.
