# Bloomy v1.9.9

A persistent Action History makes Bloomy's actions easier to understand.

- Open **Diagnostics → Action History** to search and filter actions, reported jobs, and base rewards.
- See manual, automatic, and swap-triggered nudges with their outcomes, plus model changes, provider controls, hosting, cooling, and related settings actions.
- Interrupted attempts remain visible after an app restart. Skipped automatic nudges record a reason without filling history on every telemetry poll.
- Reported account earnings retain model, earning ID, time, token counts, and amount. These records are account-wide, may be incomplete, and do not imply a nudge caused later work. Base rewards are clearly distinguished from jobs.
- History stays on this Mac, with up to 30 days or 5,000 entries. Credentials, provider keys, prompts, responses, and raw diagnostic errors are excluded. Recording failures are visible.

Action recording begins with this update; previous app actions cannot be reconstructed. Recent account earnings may be backfilled from the server's available history.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.
