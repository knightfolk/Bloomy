# Bloomy v1.9.12

The Models page now uses compact cards that match the menu-bar popup. Each card puts model status and network demand first, with active and queued request counts. Measured speed and history-based earnings remain easy to scan; forecasts and detailed assumptions are available in Manage or Details.

Demand refreshes automatically while the dashboard is open. The page shows when the reading was updated and includes a refresh button. Failed or expired readings are marked stale, with retained counts clearly identified as last known.

Enabled and Available groups remain collapsible, and model selection, startup loading, download, and management controls are preserved.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.
