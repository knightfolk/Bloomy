# Bloomy v1.9.4

The macOS menu-bar companion formerly called Darkbloom Control is now **Bloomy**. It has a new compute-companion character, app icon, and menu-bar mark. The official Darkbloom provider and CLI keep their names. Existing settings, local history, bundle identity, and signed-update identity carry forward across the rename.

- **Automatic nudge**, off by default, can make a tiny request to your own warm model after 15 minutes of observed idle time (or 30 or 60 minutes). It needs a separate “my machine only” consumer key stored in the macOS Keychain and fresh account evidence showing base rewards without work. It runs only while Bloomy is open, has no paid fallback, waits at least an hour between attempts, and allows at most three attempts in 24 hours.
- **Automatic profit switching**, also off by default, can change the single model this Mac advertises using Darkbloom's graceful switch. It considers only downloaded compatible models and requires fresh demand, earnings, power, memory, and idle-state evidence. After allowing for loading, the estimated next-hour net advantage must be at least **30% and $0.05** and persist for the configured confirmation period. Hold, return-wait, and loading times are configurable; attempts are capped at three per 24 hours. Existing multi-model setups are left alone.
- The popup and dashboard continue to show provider health, model activity, recorded earnings, estimated electricity cost, forecasts, alerts, and explicit model and lifecycle controls. Built-in Chat offers an on-Mac destination and a separately gated paid network destination. Official fan-helper controls remain in Settings; Hosting controls are in the dashboard.

These estimates use observed account earnings and local Mac activity; account work on another machine can affect attribution. Neither watcher promises public jobs or future earnings. The independent recommendation journal remains observe-only. Remote iPhone pairing and control are still in development and are **not** included in this Mac release.

For upgrading from Darkbloom Control, quit the old app before opening `Bloomy.app`. The release download is for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer. **Distribution signing and notarization status must be confirmed against the final v1.9.4 artifact before publication.**
