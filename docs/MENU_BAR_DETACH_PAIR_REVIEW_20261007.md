# Native menu-ring detach pair — October 7, 2026

A separate counterbalanced attached/detached comparison passes all sixteen
cases. The fresh ordinary fifteen-case gate still fails genuine cover and both
reopening cases (12/15), with Models and Charts passing. Detachment alone is
not sufficient to explain failure under the new probe's cadence. This is not
a production fix or a replacement release gate.

## Comparison and limits

`--detach-history-proof` compiles an independent, guarded helper. Two matched
96-point opaque borderless windows contain the unchanged production native
activity view. One stays attached; the other is removed and reinserted. A
second round reverses positions and execution order. Each arm checks baseline,
automatic post-treatment recovery, normal reopen and rapid reopen. All four
targets remain retained until cleanup, preventing address reuse from being
mistaken for stable identity.

Each accepted angle requires actual native visibility, unhidden attached view,
unchanged geometry/identity, the single production infinite 1.4-second rotation
and system Reduce Motion off. Compositor observations before and after a
1.6-second hold require angle advances greater than 0.1 radians. There are no
forced draws, transaction flushes or recovery calls. After detach and reopen,
configuration is not reapplied to rescue motion.

Review rejected a preparation call after detach that could mask failed recovery,
and a weaker angle-eligibility predicate. Both were corrected before the
reviewed artifact was executed. Two earlier compiled candidates were never
launched and are not runtime evidence. Normal builds omit the new definition
and retain the original motion helper byte-for-byte.

This probe deliberately gives post-detach motion time to reach the compositor,
including a full rotation hold. The original gate proceeds from recovery of a
model-layer animation key directly into close. Thus the result does not rule
out a detach-to-immediate-close transaction interaction. Earlier hide/order-out,
cover and drawing history also differ. Isolate these factors before changing
production recovery or assigning causality.
